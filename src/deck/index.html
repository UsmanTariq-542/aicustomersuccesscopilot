The Problem

  • CSMs manually re-listen to calls — an hour of call time per account per week that nobody gets back
  • Churn signals get buried in transcripts and lost in the noise of a 40-minute conversation
  • Follow-up emails are written from memory hours later, missing context and nuance
  • Nothing is tracked across a book of 15–30 accounts — no risk heatmap, no trend, no history

Meet Priya

A CSM who needs speed without losing control.

  • CSM at a 40–120 person B2B SaaS company
  • Owns 15–30 accounts, each with a unique story and personality
  • No technical background — needs a tool, not a query language
  • Needs to move fast, but cannot afford to send the wrong message to a customer

Why It’s Different

Human-in-the-loop approval gate — nothing goes to the customer without the CSM signing off
Risk override tracked as a first-class data point — not silently overwritten, “modified from original” is preserved
Full pipeline — speech-to-text → structured LLM extraction → generated artifacts (email, tasks, risk score), not a single prompt wrapper
